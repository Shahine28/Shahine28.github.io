## Context

This project was created during the JV 3D Week (a game jam merging two disciplines) at IIM. Inspired by the SCP series, it blends exploration and horror to deliver an immersive experience. Developed in just five days using Unreal Engine 5, it allowed me to work on gameplay mechanics and atmospheric design.

## Team

- 2 Game Programmers
- 1 Game Designer
- 1 Game Artist
- 5 3D ARTIsts
- 1 Producer

## Tasks

- AI creation: Pathfinding with navigation history to make the AI retrace its steps, dynamic navigation range.
- Integration of anamorphosis puzzles related to the mysterious rooms with light functions.
- Management of visual and sound effects to enhance the oppressive atmosphere. Post-process.
