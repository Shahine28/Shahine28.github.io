## Context

This game was developed in 3 days during the Godfather, a game jam bringing together all years from the Video Game department of my school. Organised by the heads of the department, this event marks a friendly start to the year, encouraging interactions among students. The theme of this jam was to merge video gaming with real-life roleplay, creating a unique and immersive collaborative experience.

## Team

- 1 Lead Programmer
- 2 Game Programmers
- 3 Game Designers
- 2 Game Artists

## Tasks

- **Enemies**: spawn manager, enemy behavior and their UI
- **Bonuses and maluses**: full system, from the general's decision to its in-game activation
- **Inputs**: gamepad controls for the player, keyboard controls for the general (the real-life Game Master)
- **Player**: every mechanic of the player character
