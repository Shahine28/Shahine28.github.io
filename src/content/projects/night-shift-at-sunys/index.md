## Context

This game represents the culmination of my learning on Unreal Engine 5, created entirely in Blueprint in just one month. This project not only allowed me to consolidate my skills but also successfully validated my second year at IIM, showcasing my mastery of the tool and my creativity

## Team

- 2 Game Programmers
- 2 Game Designers
- 3 Game Artists
- 1 production manager

## Tasks

- Complete programming of AI behaviour: patrolling, stimuli detection, pursuit, screamers, scanning mechanics, behaviour tree management, and NavMesh management.
- Sound object detection by the AI.
- AI interaction with objects.
- Management of safe zones and AI behaviour within these areas.
- Development of the complete mechanics of lockers (hiding spots).
- Creation of technical art with post-processing to enhance the atmosphere.
- Variations of footstep sounds depending on the surfaces walked on.
