## Context

I completed this project during my third-year Bachelor of Game Programming internship at the Level Up Com agency, in collaboration with Les Dévalideuses, a feminist disability rights group fighting against ableism. The goal was to translate their activism into a fun and accessible experience.

## Team

- 1 Game Programmer
- 2 Game Designers
- 5 Game Artists

## Tasks

- Architecture & Core (Unity / C#): Complete end-to-end technical development, State Machine, and structuring of the gameplay loop into three phases.
- Tooling & Systems: Creation of a custom Dialogue Graph tool (Editor Scripting), a data-driven customer feedback system, and progression management (upgrades).
- Algorithms & AI: Implementation of pathfinding (Dijkstra's algorithm) and a dynamic and procedural NPC spawning system.
- Accessibility (A11y) & UI: Development of advanced accessibility options (controls, feedback), and integration of fully controller-navigable menus.
- Tech Lead & Management: Sprint organization, cross-departmental technical communication (Art, Design, Production), and oversight of the asset integration pipeline.
